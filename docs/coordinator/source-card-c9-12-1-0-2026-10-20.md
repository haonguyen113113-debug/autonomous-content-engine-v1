## C-09 data contract

| Slot | Contract mapping | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `src-title` | no planner source, `authored_text` | Keeps authored `NGUỒN`. |
| `src-body` | `claim` only | Required; claim must be reviewed data, never derived from narration or replaced by the source name. |
| `credit` | `source` + `" • "` + `date` | Required; both values must be explicit reviewed data. No evidence fallback, hiding or partial credit. |

All three source fields are required by the selector's coverage check. The
builder falls back to a primitive, non-ready graph if any is missing. Credit is
not optional and cannot be hidden by the runtime contract.

## Planner, builder and QA behavior

- `apps/scene_graph/builder.py` composes `source_card` through the contract but
  joins `source`/`date` from explicit `graphic_data`, not evidence. The 12.0.0
  starter-mode surface is frozen separately so the old contract remains
  byte-exact.
- `apps/card_selector.py` maps the `source` attribution role to C-09 only.
  Fact-check (`BP-20`) and correction (`U-08`) semantics are blocked from C-09
  and reported as legacy/non-ready. Match-source timestamps may use C-09 only
  with an explicit replacement warning; they do not claim MR-12's grammar.
- Source readiness now includes plan-time text fit, rendered-inspection state
  and owner approval. `production_ready` stays false while review is pending.
- `apps/measurement/render_qa.py` requires visible `src-body` bound to `claim`
  and visible `credit` bound to both `source` and `date`. C-09 returns
  `NEEDS_REVIEW` until rendered inspection/owner approval is recorded; it never
  returns a visual `PASS` from graph-level checks alone.
- Text fit measures the claim and joined credit at the actual planned/frozen
  font size. The fixture's 82-character claim still fails; the short
  representative claim passes text fit but remains review-pending.
