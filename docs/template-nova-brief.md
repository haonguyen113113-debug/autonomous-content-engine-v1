# Template Nova — design brief (v0.9.0)

Rebuild target: exceed, not match, the reference tier. Every choice below is
observable in shipped frames, not a mood word.

## The bar (from research, Oct 2026)

1. **First frame decides.** Tifo hooks with story, FMS hooks with a neon
   claim in frame 0. Our short opens on a **hook card**: 5–8 word claim,
   condensed display type, lime rule, zero setup. No logo bumper, no greeting.
2. **One idea per Short, one number per card.** Winning stat cards show a
   single giant numeral + tiny label (FMS neon numbers, Tifo flat panels).
   Our statline gains a **hero-metric** variant: one number at 3x scale.
3. **Dark broadcast editorial, not news-farm.** Deep ink pitch, signal lime
   for passes/claims, coral for press/danger, paper-white type. VN news red/
   yellow stays out: this channel reads premium, not breaking-news.
4. **Vietnamese-first typography.** Barlow Condensed Bold for display,
   Be Vietnam Pro for text. No condensed font without full diacritics
   (Anton/Bebas break ơ ư đ) — verified in rendered frames, not font lists.
5. **Motion never sits still.** Ken Burns on every plate (shipped in 0.8.0
   renderer), fade in/out from the timeline, overlays in declared windows,
   end-card close. New in Nova: hook-card punch-in and hero-metric pop.
6. **Muted carries the message.** Lower-thirds + captions are part of the
   script, not decoration: 60%+ of Shorts starts are watched silent.
7. **Loop-aware endings.** Final line callbacks the hook; end-card keeps the
   palette so a replay feels seamless.

## Non-goals for v0.9.0

- No rebrand: channel identity (Allen Knows Ball, match-night palette
  family) stays fixed; Nova is execution quality, new graphic templates,
  and motion language — a version, not a new channel.
- No match footage: rights keep us on authored boards, licensed stills,
  and graphics. Density and typography must win instead.
- No new transitions beyond fade until slide/draw/wipe are proven in a
  report first (spec section 17: prove the slice).

## Acceptance

- 18s preview + 24s overlay proof re-rendered against 0.9.0 with all
  applicable checks PASS and limitations honestly listed.
- Hook card legible at 350px wide (phone player) with diacritics intact.
- New drafts resolve 0.9.0 by default; old runs keep pinned versions.
